# Gate 3 and Review Policy — Round 2H

Gate 3 remains before Phase 12; Gates 1 and 2 remain in their original positions. Phase 0–18 dependencies are unchanged.

## Formal readiness

Gate 3 requires completed Phase 11, approved current Protocol/upstream screening/reading/extraction, reviewed current SynthesisCorpus, four Phase 9 map assessments, Phase 10 boundary assessment, retained claims and complete normalized links. Required records need current source dependencies, supported semantic assessments and actual named human review. Coverage must include all corpus items and material synthesis propositions. Header-only files, a single unreviewed claim or manually set completion flags cannot pass.

Source, note, anchor, extraction, map, link, claim or search changes can make completed phases unready. Downstream checks recompute readiness rather than trust saved reports. Original pilot status flags are never changed to demonstrate a successful Gate; positive tests use clearly labelled fictional full approval chains.

## Three distinct levels

- Structural/source validation: IDs, schemas, hashes, coverage, link integrity and limited structured overclaim guards.
- Semantic assessment: explicit Codex/researcher judgment with evidence-by-evidence rationale; not an automatic classifier.
- Human review: separately declared approve/reject/revise/uncertain/defer/reopen/request_reading/request_discovery actions, actor, timestamp and justification.

Approval is bound to both record revision and current semantic assessment. Revisions reset approval. Rejection retires a candidate while retaining history and disallows its use as support. Deferral does not retire an unresolved issue. No CLI can authenticate a human name or prevent someone falsely declaring authorship; operating policy must supply genuine authorization.

Provisional synthesis labs refuse all human review actions and official completion. Test-only fictional approvals are permitted only in explicit synthetic official-workflow fixtures, never the real lab.

## Recovery and scope

One writer per project. Preserve unexpected generated-file edits before recovery. `synthesis-recover` regenerates views from the existing checksummed workflow journal; it cannot restore missing PDFs, infer lost scientific judgments or approve a changed Protocol.

The semantic controls here serve Phase 9–12. They do not implement Phase 17 final claim audit or replace the independent writing/audit boundary. Phase 13–15 still require current approved upstream outputs.
