# Boundary Conditions — Round 2H

Boundary analysis is a separate Phase 10 layer, not a claim adjective.

Each record carries boundary_id, prior map reference(s), dimension, context, contributing paper IDs, evidence IDs, nature_direction, strength, alternative_explanations, uncertainty, review/dependency history and one basis_level:

1. explicitly_tested: needs an explicit within_source_test_basis; presence is checked, scientific identification is not automatically established.
2. cross_study_suggested: heterogeneous contexts/designs suggest a possible scope condition; confounding remains explicit.
3. untested_hypothesis: a plausible condition for future investigation, never presented as a finding.

Dimensions are free topic-specific text; there is no default population, exposure, platform, theory or effect modifier. Historical periods, source survival, provenance, material class, institutional setting, sampling and measurement may be used when justified.

The fictional pilot uses delivery constraints and outcome timing as cross-study suggestions. It does not call them tested moderators. The real pilot does not manufacture a boundary finding from two software descriptions; their explicit design limits remain in source-specific method and claim records, with a reasoned boundary assessment.

Source/map changes stale dependent boundaries. Current semantic assessment and actual human review are required for completion; a plausible but untested statement must retain its status after approval.
