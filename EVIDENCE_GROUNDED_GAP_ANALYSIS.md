# Evidence-Grounded Gap Analysis — Round 2H

A candidate gap must explain: what the reviewed material establishes; what remains mixed, limited or uncertain; why the evidence/design/concepts cannot resolve it; and what precise research question follows. No results are presupposed.

Contract fields: gap_id/type, research_question, claim references, evidence references, established, unresolved, methodological_justification, proposed_research_question, confidence, uncertainty, coverage_basis, search_limitations, derivation, unresolved_contradictions and review/version history. Effective approved_by/date are in the current named review event, not user-controlled submit fields.

Supported types: inconsistency, methodological_limitation, conceptual_disagreement, context_population_limitation, temporal_limitation, source_limitation, mechanism_uncertainty. Valid bases: evidence_disagreement, documented_design_limit, conceptual_analysis. Provider quota, small sample of papers or unavailable downloads alone are invalid gap derivations.

Current implementation deliberately restricts scope to reviewed_corpus. Derivation begins “Within the reviewed and retrieved corpus”. Field-wide absence, originality and first-study claims are not established by this tool. Even when stronger search coverage is supplied later, a new methodological/semantic review is necessary; this round does not auto-authorize novelty claims.

Gap dependencies pin current claims and their normalized links, underlying evidence and search/citation/screening artifacts. Corrections invalidate downstream readiness. Final Phase 12 requires Gate 3 plus current semantic/human gap review and a coverage assessment.

The fictional pilot derives a provisional matched-measure comparison question from design differences and an untested mechanism, not merely from its four-paper size. The real pilot creates NO gap assertion: it explicitly defers because two preselected software papers cannot establish a defensible research gap. No-gap/insufficient-information assessments are first-class outputs, not failures to be filled with invented novelty.
