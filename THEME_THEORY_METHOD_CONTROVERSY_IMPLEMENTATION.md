# Four Comparative Maps — Round 2H

The unit of comparison is an EvidenceItem/proposition, not a paper summary. Codex or a researcher supplies the academic analysis; the CLI validates and records it.

| Map | Required judgments |
|---|---|
| Theme | label, definition, coverage, interpretation, comparability; evidence-linked comparison dimensions; support/qualification/contradiction roles |
| Theory | named concept, propositions, mechanisms, proposed/tested/mixed status, competing explanations, conceptual limits, context and evidence roles |
| Method | design, source type, case selection, analysis, measurement/interpretation, identification applicability, limitations and relevance to disagreement |
| Controversy | at least two separately supported positions, context/method differences, unresolved questions, possible explanations and evidence roles |

Each record also has the shared question, source IDs, uncertainty, research types and review/dependency metadata. No universal effect-size field or quantitative evidence hierarchy is imposed.

Theme comparisons explicitly distinguish comparable/noncomparable/uncertain. Same vocabulary does not establish equivalent measurement. Four maps can show convergence, direction differences, design/inference differences and unresolved interpretation without claiming contradiction where none exists. A controversy map must not be invented: a separately assessed `none_warranted` disposition is legitimate.

One map assessment accounts for every current map record and every included EvidenceItem. Dispositions: populated, none_warranted, deferred. A deferred map blocks official completion. Theme/method/claim maps must retain substantive records; empty theory or controversy maps need reviewed reasons.

The fictional four-paper pilot compares voluntary observational scores, imposed timed recall, qualitative accounts and a proposed feedback explanation. Positive, negative, null, mixed, secondary and uncertain evidence stays visible. The real JOSS pilot deliberately defers thematic synthesis and records no invented controversy: software design descriptions are insufficient for an empirical effect synthesis.

Unique paper counts prevent five items from one paper becoming five studies. They do NOT establish independent samples: overlapping cohorts, shared archives or common material assemblages require substantive review under the independence check. Numeric vote counting is not a consensus estimator.
