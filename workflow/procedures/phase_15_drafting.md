# Phase 15 — Section-by-Section Drafting

## Operational interface (Round 2I)

After Gate 2 and normal phase entry, use `writing-context --section ID` and inspect sources. Submit explicit Codex-authored JSON with `writing-submit --kind draft`. Ordered substantive spans carry Claim/Gap IDs, Evidence IDs and citation keys; inline syntax is `[@paper_id]`. Unresolved/transitional text needs justification. Transitions still undergo semantic audit; lexical warnings cannot establish truth.

All brief support/counter/qualifying evidence must be represented. Coverage does not establish fair interpretation. Use `writing-trace`, `writing-impact`, `writing-validate`. Revise through the same record_id, supersedes_version and stable span IDs, not hand-edited projections. Old versions persist. Writers cannot set audit status. Python does not generate prose or supplement it from model memory.

## Objective and rationale

Produce coherent review prose from approved section briefs after the evidence gate, preserving traceability and calibrated uncertainty.

## Required inputs

- Completed evidence matrix, claim map, approved outline and section briefs.
- Gate 2 pass.

## Research operations

1. Draft one section at a time following its brief and claim sequence.
2. Attribute evidence accurately and cite registered sources at the point of use.
3. Integrate supporting, contradictory, null and mixed evidence rather than serially annotating papers.
4. Preserve research-type distinctions, boundaries, limitations and allowed wording.
5. Mark unresolved citations or verification needs explicitly for audit.

## Expected artifacts

- `09_writing/DRAFT_LITERATURE_REVIEW.md`

## Evidence and provenance requirements

Every material proposition maps to a claim ID and through it to evidence IDs and exact source locations. Citations resolve to the registry.

## Academic judgment points

Synthesize heterogeneous evidence fairly, calibrate rhetoric to strength, and distinguish source authors' interpretations from the review's synthesis.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Use explicit placeholders for unresolved verification; weaken or remove prose when the evidence does not support the planned claim.

## Prohibited actions

Do not invent citations, fill gaps with model memory, upgrade association to causation, erase counterevidence, or draft before Gate 2 passes.

## Completion requirements

All approved sections are drafted, claims and citations are traceable, limitations remain visible, and unresolved items are enumerated for audit.
