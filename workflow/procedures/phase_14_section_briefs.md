# Phase 14 — Section Briefs

## Operational interface (Round 2I)

Use `writing-context`, inspect linked sources, and author one brief per section via `writing-submit --kind brief`. Required Claims/Gap IDs must exactly match the outline. Retain all supporting, contradictory and qualifying normalized links, known evidence IDs, citation keys and cannot_claim restrictions. Unknown/stale/unapproved formal Claims are rejected; unresolved issues are not filled from memory.

Only actual human `writing-review --human-attestation` can approve a brief. `writing-completion --phase 14` requires every current approved brief. See `docs/writing-tooling.md`.

## Objective and rationale

Create an evidence plan for every substantive section before prose drafting, reducing unsupported narrative drift and making counterevidence visible.

## Required inputs

- Approved review outline, claim map and relevant synthesis artifacts.

## Research operations

1. State each section's question, function and intended conclusion.
2. List claim IDs, supporting evidence IDs, contradictory evidence, boundaries and uncertainty language.
3. Specify the order of comparison or explanation and required transitions.
4. Identify citation, table or figure needs and unresolved checks.
5. Record what the section must not claim.

## Expected artifacts

- `09_writing/SECTION_BRIEFS.md`

## Evidence and provenance requirements

Every planned substantive statement resolves through a claim ID to evidence. Narrative or contextual material that lacks evidence is marked for verification.

## Academic judgment points

Balance evidence types, choose representative examples without cherry-picking, and decide where methodological differences preclude direct comparison.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Flag sections lacking adequate evidence rather than filling them with generic prose. Return to architecture or claims through a recorded decision when necessary.

## Prohibited actions

Do not draft findings from memory, omit adverse evidence for flow, or introduce sources absent from the registry.

## Completion requirements

Every substantive outline section has an auditable brief with claims, evidence, counterevidence, limits and prohibited wording.
