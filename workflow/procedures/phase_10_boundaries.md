# Phase 10 — Boundary-Condition Analysis

## Executable contract (Round 2H)

During active Phase 10, use `synthesis-submit --kind boundary`. Include evidence_ids, earlier theme/method/theory/controversy record_refs, dimension, context, nature_direction, strength, alternative_explanations and uncertainty.

Use exactly one basis_level: explicitly_tested, cross_study_suggested or untested_hypothesis. An explicitly_tested record also needs within_source_test_basis; the CLI checks its presence, not whether the proposed interaction is scientifically identified. Codex and the human reviewer must inspect the actual design. Do not promote cross-study heterogeneity to causal moderation.

Record a boundary map assessment even when no defensible boundary is warranted. Run semantic assessment, request genuine human review and check `synthesis-completion --phase 10`. Source or Phase 9 changes require renewed dependency checks; no automatic approval or advancement.

## Objective and rationale

Define where synthesized patterns do and do not apply by examining context, population, period, geography, material class, source base, method and theoretical assumptions.

## Required inputs

- Phase 9 synthesis maps.
- Evidence matrix and source-specific limitations.

## Research operations

1. Compare evidence across relevant contextual dimensions defined by the protocol and records.
2. Identify conditions under which findings, arguments or propositions change, weaken or cannot be compared.
3. Separate evidenced boundaries from plausible but untested ones.
4. Identify representation gaps created by search, access, preservation or method.
5. Link each stated boundary to evidence IDs, source limitations or an explicit coverage analysis.

## Expected artifacts

- `07_synthesis/BOUNDARY_CONDITIONS.md`

## Evidence and provenance requirements

Boundaries cite their evidence basis and scope. Unknown applicability is recorded as unknown, not as a negative finding.

## Academic judgment points

Judge which dimensions are analytically meaningful, when studies are sufficiently comparable, and how source survival or selection shapes apparent patterns.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Label underdetermined boundaries and competing explanations. Return to evidence extraction if contextual fields needed for an important comparison are missing but recoverable.

## Prohibited actions

Do not generalize beyond represented contexts, invent subgroup differences, or treat lack of accessible evidence as evidence of no effect or no history.

## Completion requirements

Major applicability limits, counterconditions, unknowns and their traceable bases are documented for use in claim calibration.
