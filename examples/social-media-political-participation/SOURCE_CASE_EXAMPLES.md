## Demonstration claim boundary

An illustrative cross-sectional association could support wording limited to an association within the observed sample. It could not support causal wording, an unmeasured outcome, a universal population, or a platform-specific effect not studied.

The source's example claim map allows wording such as "associated with" or "linked to" and prohibits "causes", "produces", or "leads to" when the design is non-causal.

Its synthetic claim illustration states that politically oriented social-media use is consistently associated with higher online political expression and points to placeholder evidence IDs `SM003-E01`, `SM017-E01`, and `SM026-E04`. It then limits the statement to mainly survey evidence and student/young-adult samples. Because no underlying papers are provided or verified, the claim is not evidence and must not be reused.
