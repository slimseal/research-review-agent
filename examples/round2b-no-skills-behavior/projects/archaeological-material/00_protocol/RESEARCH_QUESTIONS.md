# Research Questions

## Primary question

What can microwear analysis of ground-stone tools contribute to interpretations of plant-processing practices in early farming settlements?

## Subquestions

1. Which wear attributes and analytical procedures support proposed plant-processing functions?
2. How are experimental reference collections and alternative worked materials used to test attribution?
3. How do raw material, surface preservation, reuse and depositional context affect interpretability?
4. What can tool-level functional inference support about settlement practices, and which steps require independent contextual evidence?
