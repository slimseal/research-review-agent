## Concept definitions

- **ground-stone tools:** Tools shaped or used through grinding, pecking or abrasion, including grinding slabs and handstones; exclude chipped-stone-only assemblages
- **microwear analysis:** Microscopic surface observations used for functional interpretation; record resolution, surface preparation and criteria rather than assume all polish is diagnostic
- **plant-processing practices:** Actions such as grinding, pounding or dehusking plant materials; taxa and action specificity require independent warrant
- **early farming settlement:** Settlement context with regionally explicit evidence of early cultivation or farming; no universal date boundary

## Concept blocks

- **ground-stone tools:** ground stone, ground-stone, grinding stone, quern, handstone
- **microwear analysis:** microwear, use wear, use-wear, micropolish, surface texture
- **plant-processing practices:** plant processing, plant-processing, grinding, pounding, dehusking
- **early farming settlement:** early farming, early agricultural, Neolithic, settlement

## Proposed search strategies

- Combine ground-stone synonyms with microwear terms; use plant-processing and early-farming variants for targeted searches without requiring those interpretations in every broad query
- Plan reference tracing for experimental comparison methods and linked excavation reports, separating analogical references from settlement cases

## Inclusion criteria

- Original microwear investigation of ground-stone tools from contextually identified early farming settlements with traceable methods and functional reasoning
- Experimental ground-stone studies testing plant and plausible alternative contact materials, retained as analogical reference rather than settlement evidence
- Associated residues or botanical evidence only when their relationship to the analyzed tools or context is explicit

## Exclusion criteria

- Chipped-stone-only analyses and macroscopic typology without microscopic functional evidence
- Unprovenienced artifacts where settlement linkage cannot be established; classify uncertainty before exclusion
- Plant-processing assertions based solely on tool shape, and experiments lacking relevant ground-stone surfaces

## Evidence extension fields

Universal fields are fixed by the kit. The following fields are specific to this draft or approved protocol:

- `material_class` (string, required=false): Tool form, lithology and analyzed surface characteristics
- `provenience` (string, required=false): Site, unit and artifact identifier where reported
- `stratigraphic_context` (string, required=false): Depositional association and relation to settlement activities
- `chronology` (string, required=false): Regional dating framework and early-farming attribution
- `dating_method` (string, required=false): Direct or associated dating and uncertainty
- `context_integrity` (string, required=false): Disturbance, reuse and association reliability
- `taphonomic_considerations` (string, required=false): Weathering, burial alteration, cleaning and preservation effects
- `analytical_technique` (string, required=false): Magnification, instrument, surface preparation and wear recording criteria
- `assemblage_relation` (string, required=false): Tool-level observation versus assemblage sampling and spatial association
- `comparative_reference` (string, required=false): Experimental material, worked substances, task and match limitations
