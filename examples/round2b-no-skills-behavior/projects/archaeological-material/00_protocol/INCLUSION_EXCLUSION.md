# Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria

Criteria version: `0.1-draft`

## Include

- Original microwear investigation of ground-stone tools from contextually identified early farming settlements with traceable methods and functional reasoning
- Experimental ground-stone studies testing plant and plausible alternative contact materials, retained as analogical reference rather than settlement evidence
- Associated residues or botanical evidence only when their relationship to the analyzed tools or context is explicit

## Exclude

- Chipped-stone-only analyses and macroscopic typology without microscopic functional evidence
- Unprovenienced artifacts where settlement linkage cannot be established; classify uncertainty before exclusion
- Plant-processing assertions based solely on tool shape, and experiments lacking relevant ground-stone surfaces

Uncertain records must remain uncertain until the missing eligibility information is available.
