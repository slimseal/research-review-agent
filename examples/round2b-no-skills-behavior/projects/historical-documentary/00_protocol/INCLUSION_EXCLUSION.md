# Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria

Criteria version: `0.1-draft`

## Include

- Records with identifiable municipal provenance, creation date within 1939–1950 and substantive treatment of household fuel allocation or scarcity in a British city
- Reproductions or transcriptions only with traceable provenance and acknowledged mediation
- Historical scholarship may guide contextualization and source criticism but cannot substitute for inspection of a cited primary record

## Exclude

- Industrial-only fuel accounts or national policy with no municipal household connection
- Undated or unattributable extracts without recoverable provenance, retained as uncertain until resolved
- Records outside the period as primary evidence of contemporary framing; recollections treated separately rather than as contemporaneous municipal statements

Uncertain records must remain uncertain until the missing eligibility information is available.
