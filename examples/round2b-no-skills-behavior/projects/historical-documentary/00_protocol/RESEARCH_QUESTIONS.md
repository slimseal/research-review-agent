# Research Questions

## Primary question

How did municipal rationing records frame household fuel scarcity in British cities between 1939 and 1950?

## Subquestions

1. What administrative categories and language were used to represent household fuel scarcity?
2. Who produced the records, for which audiences and administrative purposes?
3. How did representations differ across document genres, cities and wartime/postwar intervals?
4. Which households and experiences are visible, filtered or absent, and how does record survival limit comparison?
