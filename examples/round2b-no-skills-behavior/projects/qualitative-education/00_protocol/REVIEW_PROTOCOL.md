# Review Protocol

Status: **draft**

## Research question

How do adult literacy tutors describe adapting feedback for multilingual learners?

## Review design

- Review type: `rigorous_narrative_or_thematic`
- Domain: `qualitative education`
- Protocol version: `0.1-draft`
- Schema version: `2.0.0`
- Research types: `qualitative_empirical`

## Scope

- units_of_analysis: Tutor accounts and situated feedback episodes in adult literacy teaching
- phenomena_or_topics: Tutors’ descriptions of adapting feedback for learners using multiple languages
- relationships_or_questions: What do tutors count as feedback and adaptation in adult literacy learning?, How do tutors explain language choice, timing, modality and directness of feedback?, What tensions or constraints do tutors describe, and what discrepant accounts occur?, How do program setting, tutor experience and language repertoire shape the transferability of accounts?
- contexts: Community, voluntary and formal adult literacy provision; retain tutor training and learner language context
- geographic_scope: No geographic restriction proposed; compare institutional and language-policy settings
- temporal_scope: No publication-year restriction proposed; record fieldwork dates and program changes
- languages: Proposed initial English screening; other languages flagged for human translation decision
- source_types: Original qualitative interview, focus-group, observational and reflective accounts with tutor voice identifiable

## Subquestions

1. What do tutors count as feedback and adaptation in adult literacy learning?
2. How do tutors explain language choice, timing, modality and directness of feedback?
3. What tensions or constraints do tutors describe, and what discrepant accounts occur?
4. How do program setting, tutor experience and language repertoire shape the transferability of accounts?

## Concept definitions

- **adult literacy tutors:** Paid or volunteer educators supporting adult reading and writing; not school-age teachers unless adult provision is separable
- **adaptive feedback:** Responses to learner work or understanding adjusted in language, timing, modality or interaction; exclude generic differentiation with no feedback episode
- **multilingual learners:** Adults using or learning across multiple languages; do not presume migration status or treat multilingualism as a deficit

## Concept blocks

- **adult literacy tutors:** adult literacy tutor, adult basic education teacher, literacy volunteer
- **adaptive feedback:** feedback, response to writing, corrective feedback, formative feedback
- **multilingual learners:** multilingual, plurilingual, additional language, language repertoire

## Proposed search strategies

- Combine adult-literacy educator terms with feedback terms; use multilingual terminology as an additional family to avoid assuming all authors use the same label
- Plan reference tracing and program terminology expansion after discovery; do not require a qualitative-method label in every query

## Inclusion criteria

- Original qualitative material about tutors in adult literacy provision describing feedback adaptations for multilingual learners
- Mixed-method studies only for separately identifiable qualitative tutor accounts
- Learner or observational material may contextualize tutor accounts but must remain attributed to its own perspective

## Exclusion criteria

- School-child-only teaching or higher-education disciplinary writing without adult literacy provision
- Learner-only accounts with no tutor description relevant to the question
- Quantitative effectiveness scores without qualitative accounts; generic multilingual pedagogy with no feedback adaptation

## Evidence extension fields

Universal fields are fixed by the kit. The following fields are specific to this draft or approved protocol:

- `participants_or_cases` (string, required=false): Tutor role, experience, repertoire and program characteristics as reported
- `sampling_strategy` (string, required=false): Case selection and whose accounts are absent
- `data_collection` (string, required=false): Interview, focus group, observation or reflective material and elicitation context
- `analytic_approach` (string, required=false): Source analytic method and how interpretations were developed
- `researcher_positioning` (string, required=false): Relationship to tutors and institutions where reported
- `participant_voice` (string, required=false): Tutor quotation with exact locator, separated from author paraphrase
- `theme_or_interpretation` (string, required=false): Source interpretation distinguished from later reviewer interpretation
- `negative_case` (string, required=false): Discrepant account or explicit absence of its reporting
- `transferability_context` (string, required=false): Program, learner-language context and limits on transfer
