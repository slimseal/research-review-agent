# Research Questions

## Primary question

How do adult literacy tutors describe adapting feedback for multilingual learners?

## Subquestions

1. What do tutors count as feedback and adaptation in adult literacy learning?
2. How do tutors explain language choice, timing, modality and directness of feedback?
3. What tensions or constraints do tutors describe, and what discrepant accounts occur?
4. How do program setting, tutor experience and language repertoire shape the transferability of accounts?
