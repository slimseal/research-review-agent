# Research Questions

## Primary question

How is schedule predictability associated with job retention intentions among hourly retail workers?

## Subquestions

1. Which dimensions of schedule predictability are measured, and over what recall period?
2. How are intentions to stay or leave operationalized, including their time horizon?
3. How do adjusted and unadjusted associations vary by study design and worker or workplace context?
4. What selection, reverse-causation and measurement limitations constrain interpretation?
