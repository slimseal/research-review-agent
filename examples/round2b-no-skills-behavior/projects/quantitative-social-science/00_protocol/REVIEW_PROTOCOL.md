# Review Protocol

Status: **draft**

## Research question

How is schedule predictability associated with job retention intentions among hourly retail workers?

## Review design

- Review type: `rigorous_narrative_or_thematic`
- Domain: `quantitative social science`
- Protocol version: `0.1-draft`
- Schema version: `2.0.0`
- Research types: `quantitative_empirical`

## Scope

- units_of_analysis: Hourly retail workers; worker-level measurements nested within stores or employers
- phenomena_or_topics: Schedule predictability and intention to remain with the current employer
- relationships_or_questions: Which dimensions of schedule predictability are measured, and over what recall period?, How are intentions to stay or leave operationalized, including their time horizon?, How do adjusted and unadjusted associations vary by study design and worker or workplace context?, What selection, reverse-causation and measurement limitations constrain interpretation?
- contexts: Retail employment; distinguish store formats, employment contracts and labor-market settings
- geographic_scope: No geographic restriction proposed; retain jurisdiction and labor regulation context
- temporal_scope: No publication-year restriction proposed; record data-collection period and distinguish contemporaneous from longitudinal measurements
- languages: Proposed initial English screening; other languages flagged for human translation decision
- source_types: Original quantitative surveys, panel studies and workplace evaluations with separable retail-worker results

## Subquestions

1. Which dimensions of schedule predictability are measured, and over what recall period?
2. How are intentions to stay or leave operationalized, including their time horizon?
3. How do adjusted and unadjusted associations vary by study design and worker or workplace context?
4. What selection, reverse-causation and measurement limitations constrain interpretation?

## Concept definitions

- **schedule predictability:** Advance notice, stability of scheduled hours, and employer-initiated last-minute changes; distinguish predictability from total hours and worker-controlled flexibility
- **retention intentions:** Stated intention to stay with the current employer; turnover intention is coded with its original direction and is not observed retention
- **hourly retail workers:** Employees paid by the hour in retail establishments; mixed-sector samples need separately extractable retail results

## Concept blocks

- **schedule predictability:** schedule predictability, advance notice, schedule instability, last-minute schedule changes
- **retention intentions:** intention to stay, retention intention, turnover intention, intention to quit
- **hourly retail workers:** hourly workers, retail employees, retail workers

## Proposed search strategies

- Combine scheduling synonyms with retail-worker terms, then add intention terms as a precision variant; compare coverage before choosing final syntax
- Plan backward and forward tracing from eligible sources only after authorized discovery; retain all association directions

## Inclusion criteria

- Original quantitative evidence jointly measuring schedule predictability and current-employer retention or turnover intentions among hourly retail workers
- Mixed-sector or mixed-pay samples only when relevant subgroup results are extractable
- Cross-sectional and longitudinal designs retained separately, with measurement and adjustment information recoverable from full text

## Exclusion criteria

- Actual turnover alone without an intention measure
- Hours quantity, wages or flexibility alone without a predictability dimension
- Non-retail or salaried-only samples without an eligible subgroup; commentary without original quantitative evidence

## Evidence extension fields

Universal fields are fixed by the kit. The following fields are specific to this draft or approved protocol:

- `population` (string, required=false): Retail role, hourly-pay eligibility, jurisdiction and sample selection
- `study_design` (string, required=false): Cross-sectional, panel or evaluation design and measurement timing
- `measurement` (string, required=false): Schedule dimensions, intention scale wording, recall period and outcome direction
- `model_specification` (string, required=false): Adjustment set, clustering, missing-data handling and model scale
- `effect_size` (string, required=false): Reported association and scale only; no conversion without sufficient source information
- `uncertainty` (string, required=false): Reported interval or standard error and its level
- `identification_basis` (string, required=false): Association, prediction or explicit causal-identification assumptions; never infer causality from adjustment
