# Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria

Criteria version: `0.1-draft`

## Include

- Original quantitative evidence jointly measuring schedule predictability and current-employer retention or turnover intentions among hourly retail workers
- Mixed-sector or mixed-pay samples only when relevant subgroup results are extractable
- Cross-sectional and longitudinal designs retained separately, with measurement and adjustment information recoverable from full text

## Exclude

- Actual turnover alone without an intention measure
- Hours quantity, wages or flexibility alone without a predictability dimension
- Non-retail or salaried-only samples without an eligible subgroup; commentary without original quantitative evidence

Uncertain records must remain uncertain until the missing eligibility information is available.
