This curated public demonstration contains paraphrased research results and product output, **not PDFs, raw abstracts, private approvals or complete replayable research projects**. It is not a model answer for future topics.

1. **Formal research pilot:** approved question and search planning, 150 retrieved metadata occurrences, 147 provisional Paper IDs after initial deduplication, and 32 additional IDs from citation expansion. The 179 machine-screening recommendations were 2 continue / 129 exclude / 48 uncertain. No final paper-level human screening decisions are implied. Phase 6 and Gate 1 remained incomplete/blocked.

## Research question used in this demonstration

> How is urban green-space exposure associated with depressive symptoms among adolescents aged 10–19, and how do reported associations vary across exposure measures and study designs?

This case is confined to the demonstration. The core workflow derives concepts, eligibility and evidence fields from each new question and approved protocol.

## Real source and reading

Larsen, S. R., Rakesh, D., Whittle, S., Allen, N. B., Enticott, P. G., & Mygind, L. (2025). Residential greenness and adolescent mental health trajectories: A longitudinal pre-registered study. *Environmental Research, 283*, 122150. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.envres.2025.122150).

The private source copy was downloaded by the user through King's Research Portal and imported locally; the Agent's earlier online acquisition attempt was not successful. It is not bundled here. DocumentVersion `DV-D39A8410E2EF44E1925E`; SHA-256 `90e69d68cb609359bfce1499ef23b73291310d17b8833142e0578822f17cdd6e`. These identifiers allow source comparison, not access to private records.

The inspected copy has 10 physical pages: page 1 is an institutional cover; the article begins at PDF page 2. PDF pages 3–10 have printed page numbers 2–9. This offset matters for every locator below. The institutional cover and article contain different rights wording; publishing this summary does not grant permission to redistribute the PDF.

Reading Notes identified a prospective observational OADS cohort in metropolitan Melbourne, 245 participants, and four approximate assessment ages of 12, 15, 17 and 19. Exposure was baseline residential NDVI, with alternative buffers and park-distance sensitivity analyses. Depression was measured with CES-D, separately from anxiety and CBCL externalizing symptoms. Robust mixed models and adjustment do not establish causality. Exact age ranges and urban-core versus suburban eligibility were not finally adjudicated.

## What is deliberately not public

Private project directories, authentic approval signatures, raw provider payloads, downloaded full text, source quotations/parsed page bodies, environment values, GB-scale journals and local filesystem paths are excluded. No historical logs are redacted and then represented as original checksummed history. Public artifacts are read-only summaries and cannot reproduce or authorize a formal review by themselves.
