# Single-study evidence-based writing sample

Real-source engineering demonstration; not a completed multi-study review. No independent scientific audit or final human approval has been completed. The four substantive paragraphs below are reproduced unchanged from the existing isolated test. Citation key `[@P0000107]` refers to the single real source below.

In the Orygen Adolescent Development Study, baseline residential greenness did not show a consistent association with depressive-symptom trajectories. Alternative NDVI buffers and park-distance analyses did not resolve this uncertainty. These null-compatible reports should not be interpreted as evidence of equivalence or absence of an association. The printed main-model depression interval is internally inconsistent with its point estimate and the null-crossing description and figure; its bounds therefore remain unverified here [@P0000107].

Exploratory depression NDVI-by-age-by-sex estimates were 3.28 (95% CI -0.56 to 7.13; N=241) without adjustment and 3.46 (95% CI -0.58 to 7.51; N=199) with adjustment. Both intervals include zero, leaving sex-related moderation uncertain rather than demonstrating that it is absent [@P0000107].

The exploratory externalizing-symptom interaction changed from p=0.038 before adjustment to p=0.077 after adjustment. Because this analysis concerns CBCL externalizing symptoms, it cannot establish a CES-D depressive-symptom benefit [@P0000107].

Interpretation is further limited by baseline NDVI as a proxy for actual nature experience, omitted residential moves, residual confounding and missing symptom observations. CES-D missing counts were 18, 39, 71 and 82 across the four waves in the enrolled cohort of 245. These are limitations of this source, not evidence of a field-wide research gap [@P0000107].
